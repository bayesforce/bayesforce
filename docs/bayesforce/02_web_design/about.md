## 3. Section Blueprints & Copy Details

### Section 1: Hero
- **Headline (H1):** *Systems should carry more of the machinery of an organization so its people can carry more of its ambition.*
- **Subcopy:** BayesForce is an AI capability engineering firm. We help high-growth enterprises eliminate operational drag by embedding autonomous AI coworkers, data pipelines, and governance controls directly into production workflows.

### Section 2: The 5 Core Operating Principles
1. **Work Before Technology:** We start by understanding the operational friction, the handoffs, and the business logic—not by pitching an AI model.
2. **Make Technology Invisible:** The best AI coworkers operate inside Slack, CRMs, ERPs, and internal tools where work already happens, without forcing users into yet another standalone portal.
3. **Earn Autonomy:** We never assume black-box autonomy on day one. AI systems begin in shadow mode, graduate to human supervision, and earn autonomy through verified evaluation benchmarks.
4. **Prove the Delta:** We measure everything. Every workflow engagement is judged by verifiable business metrics: cycle time compression, error reduction, touchless rates, and unlocked human capacity.
5. **Design for Ownership:** We co-engineer capabilities within your codebase and cloud infrastructure, transferring complete operational ownership and know-how to your internal team.

### Section 3: The 11-Stage Delivery Model
Detailed breakdown of the lifecycle:
- *Trigger → Ingestion → Document Understanding → Context Assembly → Deterministic Validation → Reasoning & Planning → System Execution → Structured Output → Human Review Boundaries → Immutable Recording → Continuous Delta Measurement.*
