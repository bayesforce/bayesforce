## 3. Section Blueprints & Copy Details

### Section 1: Hero
- **Headline (H1):** *We hire exceptional builders, not rigid job titles.*
- **Subcopy:**
  > *"We are always open to hire exceptional talent. Go through the website, understand our worldview, and tell us what value you can bring to Bayesforce."*
- We care about how you think, your technical craft, and your ability to take complex, messy real-world problems and turn them into elegant, reliable systems.

### Section 2: Core Engineering Values
1. **First-Principles Thinking:** Deconstruct operational problems to their fundamental units before choosing an algorithm.
2. **Extreme Craftsmanship:** Whether writing a parser, designing an eval suite, or building a UI transition, details matter.
3. **High Velocity with Rigor:** Move fast, but test continuously with golden datasets and deterministic checks.

### Section 3: Open-Intent Application Flow
- Unlike corporate hiring queues with rigid drop-down filters, candidates submit:
  - Who you are and what you care about.
  - Links to what you've built (GitHub, papers, projects, codebases).
  - Your perspective on the BayesForce thesis and where you can create massive leverage.
