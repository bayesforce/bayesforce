## 3. Detailed Section Blueprints

### Section 1: Hero (Obsidian Black Surface)
- **Container:** Full-bleed Obsidian Black (`#11151B`) with radial cobalt ambient spotlight (`#013EFA` at 12% opacity) and subtle animated grid lines.
- **Eyebrow Badge:** `AI CAPABILITY ENGINEERING` (Pill badge with cobalt border and glowing backdrop).
- **H1 Headline:**
  > **Make More Happen.**
- **Sub-Headline Copy:**
  > Systems should carry more of the machinery of an organization so its people can carry more of its ambition. We engineer production AI capabilities directly into your existing operational workflows.
- **Action Buttons:**
  - `[Talk to Bayesforce]` (Primary: Solid Cobalt Blue `#013EFA`, white text, hover lift) → `/talk`
  - `[Explore Workflows]` (Secondary: Obsidian border with hover glow) → `/workflows/revenue-operations`
- **Proof Strip / Trust Metrics:**
  - `4 Core Capabilities` · `8 Operational Business Functions` · `11-Stage Governed Lifecycle` · `Zero Replatforming Required`

### Section 2: Operational Drag (Surface Light)
- **Container:** Surface White (`#FFFFFF`) with neutral border accents.
- **Eyebrow:** `THE OPERATIONAL REALITY`
- **H2 Title:** *Work gets stuck in the connective tissue between teams and tools.*
- **Body Copy:** Every growing organization develops operational drag: people chasing context, systems that don't talk to each other, manual data reconciliations, and constant human handoffs simply to keep routine processes moving.
- **3-Card Interactive Breakdown:**
  1. **Disconnected Systems:** Critical data fragmented across ERPs, CRMs, legacy databases, and custom tools.
  2. **Unstructured & Messy Context:** Vital records trapped in PDFs, emails, PDFs, and conversational threads.
  3. **Human Coordination Burden:** High-value knowledge workers acting as manual data glue rather than applying strategic judgment.

### Section 3: The Bayesforce Execution Model (Obsidian Dark)
- **Container:** Obsidian Black (`#11151B`).
- **Eyebrow:** `HOW WE ENGAGE`
- **H2 Title:** *The workflow is our unit of engagement.*
- **Core Concept:** We do not sell AI as a standalone dashboard, and we do not ask you to replace your existing software. We engineer an autonomous capability around a specific piece of operational work—connecting the data, deploying the coworker, establishing trust controls, and transferring ownership.
- **Execution Flow Diagram:**
  $$\text{Find High-Friction Workflow} \longrightarrow \text{Connect Data \& Systems} \longrightarrow \text{Provide Enterprise Context} \longrightarrow \text{Deploy AI Coworker} \longrightarrow \text{Enforce Human Gates} \longrightarrow \text{Measure Delta}$$
