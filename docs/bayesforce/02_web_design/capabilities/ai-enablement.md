## 3. Section Blueprints & Copy Details

### Section 1: Hero
- **Headline (H1):** *We engineer capabilities, then transfer ownership.*
- **Subcopy:** The goal of an AI engineering engagement is not permanent vendor dependency. We co-engineer alongside your team, establish standards, document architecture, and train your staff until you own and operate the capability with full confidence.
- **Visual:** The 4-Step Capability Transfer model rendered as an interactive horizontal pathway:
  $$\text{1. Understand Workflow} \longrightarrow \text{2. Co-Engineer} \longrightarrow \text{3. Train \& Transfer} \longrightarrow \text{4. Measure Delta}$$

### Section 2: Core Engineering Pillars
- **Pillar 1: Co-Engineering in Your Repo:** All code, pipelines, and evaluation harnesses are built directly within your GitHub/GitLab repositories and cloud infrastructure.
- **Pillar 2: Technical Knowledge Transfer:** Comprehensive documentation, architectural diagrams, runbooks, and pair-programming sessions with your engineers.
- **Pillar 3: Operator & Manager Training:** Equipping operational team leads with dashboards, exception queues, and feedback loop mechanisms.
- **Pillar 4: Organizational AI Literacy:** Pragmatic executive and team workshops focusing on workflow decomposition, feasibility screening, and ROI measurement.
