## 3. Section Blueprints & Copy Details

### Section 1: Hero
- **Headline (H1):** *Coordinate employee lifecycle logistics and policy inquiries without manual HR administrative burden.*
- **Subcopy:** People teams exist to support and develop talent, yet administrative coordination (intake forms, equipment provisioning, handbook inquiries, offboarding handoffs) consumes immense time. BayesForce engineers autonomous workflows around HR logistics.

### Section 2: Candidate Workflows
1. **Employee Onboarding Orchestration:** When an offer is signed in the ATS, auto-create HRIS profiles, trigger IT provisioning requests, dispatch welcome emails, and track checklist completion across departments.
2. **HR Policy & Benefits Desk:** Provide instant, accurate answers to complex benefits, parental leave, and PTO questions directly in Slack, citing the exact employee handbook section.
3. **Document & Credential Verification:** Intake identification and certification documents from new hires, check validity and expiration dates, and flag renewals.
4. **Governed Offboarding Coordination:** Ensure 100% compliance during departures by triggering access revocation, asset return shipping labels, and exit survey administration.
