## 3. Section Blueprints & Copy Details

### Section 1: Hero
- **Headline (H1):** *Resolve customer issues with full enterprise context, not scripted boilerplate responses.*
- **Subcopy:** Customer support fails when AI is treated as a superficial chat wrapper. BayesForce connects your ticketing platform to production logs, billing systems, usage databases, and past tickets—enabling coworkers to investigate root causes and draft complete resolutions.

### Section 2: Candidate Workflows
1. **Context-Driven Ticket Investigation:** Auto-assemble customer history, active subscriptions, recent errors, and telemetry logs into an internal triage note before the rep even opens the ticket.
2. **Autonomous Tier-1 Resolution:** Execute routine customer requests (e.g. permission resets, invoice reprints, status updates) with full policy compliance and audit logging.
3. **Escalation Packet Preparation:** When a ticket must be escalated to Engineering or Product, generate a structured reproduction packet with exact timestamps, user IDs, and system logs.
4. **Customer Onboarding Coordination:** Track technical milestone completion across new enterprise accounts and prompt stakeholders when tasks stall.

### Section 3: Connected Knowledge & Proof
- **Relevant Capability:** [AI Data Engineering](../capabilities/ai-data-engineering.md) & [AI Coworkers](../capabilities/ai-coworkers.md)
