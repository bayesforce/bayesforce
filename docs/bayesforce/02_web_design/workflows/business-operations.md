## 3. Section Blueprints & Copy Details

### Section 1: Hero
- **Headline (H1):** *Turn standard operating procedures into autonomous execution, removing manual coordination drag.*
- **Subcopy:** Growing companies write extensive documentation and SOPs, yet knowledge workers spend hours manually executing multi-step tasks across disconnected tools. BayesForce turns SOPs into active AI coworkers that execute internal operations reliably.

### Section 2: Candidate Workflows
1. **Internal Operations Request Desk:** Ingest internal employee requests from Slack or email, classify intent, extract required parameters, execute actions in underlying tools, and reply with status updates.
2. **Autonomous SOP Execution:** Parse documented operating procedures and execute multi-system sequences (e.g. quarterly account reassignment, inventory reconciliation, partner setup).
3. **Cross-Team Approval & Handoff Coordination:** Route conditional approvals based on business logic, nudge slow approvers, and trigger downstream tasks automatically once approved.
4. **Automated Weekly Decision Packs:** Aggregate KPIs, pipeline changes, and operational exceptions from disparate dashboards into an executive briefing summary.

### Section 3: Connected Knowledge & Proof
