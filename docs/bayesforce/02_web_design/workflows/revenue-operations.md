## 3. Section Blueprints & Copy Details

### Section 1: Hero
- **Headline (H1):** *Keep sales teams focused on conversations, not CRM data entry and lead chasing.*
- **Subcopy:** High-growth sales organizations lose 30%+ of productive selling time to manual lead enrichment, context reconstruction, and CRM administration. BayesForce engineers autonomous RevOps capabilities directly into Salesforce, HubSpot, and Slack.

### Section 2: Candidate Workflows
1. **Lead Qualification & Routing:** Ingest inbound leads from demo forms, enrich company revenue/headcount/tech-stack from public and private sources, evaluate ICP fit, and route high-intent buyers instantly.
2. **Account Research & Executive Briefings:** Synthesize recent news, 10-K filings, hiring trends, and existing CRM interactions into concise rep briefings prior to discovery calls.
3. **Continuous CRM Hygiene:** Auto-reconcile calendar meetings, email threads, and Slack conversations into structured opportunity notes and stage changes without rep friction.
4. **Deal Health & Slippage Detection:** Cross-reference customer engagement velocity against historical closed-won patterns to flag stalled deals proactively.
