## 3. Section Blueprints & Copy Details

### Section 1: Hero
- **Headline (H1):** *Accelerate contract triage and compliance audits with explicit human-in-the-loop review boundaries.*
- **Subcopy:** Legal and compliance teams are critical guardians of enterprise risk, but routine reviews slow down commercial momentum. BayesForce builds AI coworkers that parse contracts, populate security questionnaires, and audit vendor DPAs—always under the final approval of qualified legal counsel.

### Section 2: Candidate Workflows
1. **Contract Intake & Standard Clause Triage:** Ingest inbound customer or vendor redlines, cross-reference against approved playbook standard terms (indemnity caps, governing law, IP terms), highlight deviations, and draft proposed compromise clauses.
2. **Security & Compliance Questionnaire Auto-Completion:** Extract questions from incoming security assessments (SIG, CAIQ, VSA) and retrieve authoritative answers from historical compliance documentation and security whitepapers.
3. **Contract Obligation & Renewal Tracking:** Extract key dates, notice periods, price escalation formulas, and SLA penalty clauses into a centralized, queryable contract knowledge base.
4. **Vendor DPA & Subprocessor Compliance:** Audit supplier privacy agreements against GDPR, CCPA, and enterprise standards to ensure data transfer compliance.
