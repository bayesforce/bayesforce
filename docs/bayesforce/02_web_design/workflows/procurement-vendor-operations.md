## 3. Section Blueprints & Copy Details

### Section 1: Hero
- **Headline (H1):** *Accelerate vendor onboarding and contract reviews without compromising compliance or controls.*
- **Subcopy:** Vendor onboarding often stalls across procurement, security, legal, and finance. BayesForce deploys AI coworkers that orchestrate document intake, verify insurance and tax certificates, validate banking details, and update your ERP with zero manual chasing.

### Section 2: Candidate Workflows
1. **End-to-End Vendor Onboarding:** Intake supplier details via web forms or email, auto-extract tax IDs and bank remittance details, verify certificates of insurance (COI) against required coverage minimums, and provision vendor profiles in the ERP.
2. **Purchase Requisition Policy Verification:** Check inbound purchase requests against departmental budgets, approval authority matrices, and preferred vendor contracts.
3. **Multi-Vendor Quote Comparison:** Normalize unstructured vendor bids and proposals into a side-by-side comparison matrix evaluating unit pricing, delivery schedules, and payment terms.
4. **Contract-to-PO Validation:** Cross-reference final signed MSAs and SOWs against draft purchase orders to guarantee commercial terms match before PO dispatch.
