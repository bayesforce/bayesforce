## 1. Specification Framework

This document defines the progressive section-by-section wireframe, visual hierarchy, UI components, and verbatim copy for every route across the Bayesforce web platform.

Each page specification includes:
- **Route & Layout Pattern:** URL path, background themes (Obsidian Dark vs. Surface Light), container widths.
- **Section Sequence:** Ordered list of functional page blocks.
- **Visual Composition:** Visual tokens, component types, cards, badges, diagrams, and animations.
- **Verbatim Copy:** Headline, eyebrow badge, supporting copy, feature points, and CTAs.

## Page 1: Home (`/`)

### Section 1.1: Hero (Obsidian Black Surface)
- **Visual Container:** Full-bleed Obsidian Black (`#11151B`) with subtle radial gradient (`#013EFA` at 12% opacity) and precise border grid lines (`#282E38`).
- **Eyebrow Badge:**
  - *Text:* `AI CAPABILITY ENGINEERING`
  - *Style:* Pill badge, border `rgba(1, 62, 250, 0.4)`, font Outfit 12px uppercase tracking 0.1em.
- **Primary Headline (H1):**
  > **Make More Happen.**
- **Sub-Headline:**
  > Systems should carry more of the machinery of an organization so its people can carry more of its ambition. We engineer production AI capabilities directly into your existing operational workflows.
- **Call-to-Action Group:**
  - *Primary Button:* `Evaluate a Workflow` (Cobalt Blue `#013EFA` fill, Outfit font, 14px Medium)
  - *Secondary Button:* `Explore Capabilities` (Ghost button with Obsidian border `#282E38` and white text)
- **Proof / Metric Strip:**
  - `4 Core Capabilities` · `8 Business Functions Supported` · `11-Stage Governed Lifecycle` · `Zero Workflow Replatforming`

---

### Section 1.2: The Core Problem — Operational Drag (Surface Light)
- **Visual Container:** Clean White/Light Surface (`#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC`).
- **Eyebrow:** `THE OPERATIONAL REALITY`
- **Section Heading:**
  > Work gets stuck in the connective tissue between teams and tools.
- **Body Copy:**
  > Every growing organization develops operational drag: people chasing context, systems that don't talk to each other, manual data reconciliations, and constant human handoffs simply to keep routine processes moving.
- **Interactive Drag Breakdown (3 Visual Cards):**
  1. **Disconnected Systems:** Critical records split across ERPs, CRMs, spreadsheets, and ticketing tools.
  2. **Messy Information:** Key transaction context trapped in PDFs, emails, unstructured documents, and slack threads.
  3. **Human Coordination Burden:** Expensive knowledge workers acting as human glue rather than applying judgment.

---

### Section 1.3: What We Do — The Workflow Wedge (Obsidian Black)
- **Section Heading:**
  > The workflow is our unit of engagement.
- **Body Copy:**
  > We do not sell AI as a standalone layer, and we do not ask you to replace your existing software. We engineer an autonomous capability around a specific piece of operational work—connecting the data, deploying the coworker, establishing trust and controls, and transferring ownership to your team.
- **Land & Expand Visual Model:**
  > `Workflow → Adjacent Workflows → Business Function → Cross-Functional Operations`

---

### Section 1.4: The 4 Core Capabilities (Grid)
- **Capability 1:** AI Data Engineering — Pipelines & Organizational Memory ("Second Brain").
- **Capability 2:** AI Coworkers — Autonomous execution engines running across existing tech stacks.
- **Capability 3:** AI Trust & Governance — Evals, observability, cost tracking, and permission controls.
- **Capability 4:** AI Enablement — Structured knowledge transfer and team self-sufficiency.

---

### Section 1.5: The 11-Stage Delivery Model (Linear Visual Stepper)
- **Lifecycle Strip:** `Trigger → Ingest → Understand → Context → Validate → Reason → Execute → Output → Review → Record → Measure`
- **Core Callout:** *"Autonomy is earned, not assumed. Every workflow incorporates human review boundaries at consequential decision points."*

---

### Section 1.6: Final Call to Action (Hero Banner)
- **Headline:** Ready to remove operational drag from your core workflows?
- **CTA:** `Start with a Workflow Audit` → `/engage`
