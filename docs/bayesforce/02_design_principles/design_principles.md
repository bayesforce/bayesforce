# BayesForce — Design Principles

> **Cobalt Blue × Obsidian Black**  
> **Design direction:** Premium AI engineering / systems engineering  
> **Brand:** BayesForce  
> **Primary typefaces:** Outfit for display, labels and headings; Inter for body/UI text  
> **Core visual idea:** Precision engineering expressed through a high-contrast cobalt-and-obsidian system.

---

## 1. Design Intent

BayesForce should look like an **AI engineering partner**, not a generic AI consultancy, software agency, or AI SaaS startup.

The visual identity should communicate:
- **Engineering depth**
- **Technical precision**
- **Confidence**
- **Enterprise credibility**
- **Modern AI capability**
- **Systems thinking**
- **High-value execution**
- **A restrained sense of technological energy**

The identity should feel premium and technical without becoming cold or overly futuristic.

The design should avoid looking like a template assembled from common AI-brand conventions.

### Core Visual Principle

> **Obsidian provides authority. Cobalt provides intelligence and motion. White space provides clarity.**

Cobalt should be used as the **signal color**. Obsidian should establish the **foundation and seriousness** of the brand.

## 3. Color Philosophy

### 3.1 Cobalt is a signal, not a wallpaper

Cobalt should attract attention.

**Use it for:**
- Primary CTA buttons
- Links
- Active navigation
- Key statistics
- Selected states
- Small UI indicators
- Important words in headlines
- Data visualisation highlights
- System diagrams
- The BayesForce mark

Avoid making every component blue.

The strongest visual hierarchy is:
$$\text{Obsidian} \longrightarrow \text{White} \longrightarrow \text{Cobalt}$$

rather than:
$$\text{Blue} \longrightarrow \text{Blue} \longrightarrow \text{Blue}$$

### 3.2 Obsidian is the engineering foundation

Obsidian should replace conventional pure black.

Avoid using `#000000` as the primary dark brand color.

The slight blue-neutral character of `#11151B` gives the dark surfaces a more sophisticated technical feel.

**Use Obsidian for:**
- Hero backgrounds
- Dark case-study sections
- Navigation on dark pages
- System diagrams
- Footer
- High-contrast feature sections
- Technical visualisations

### 3.3 White space is part of the identity

The brand should not rely entirely on dark UI.

Light sections are important because they create contrast and make the cobalt elements feel more intentional.

A typical page can alternate:
$$\text{Light} \longrightarrow \text{Obsidian} \longrightarrow \text{Light} \longrightarrow \text{Obsidian}$$

rather than remaining dark throughout.

---

## 4. Recommended Color Ratios

For a typical website page:
- **55–65%:** White / very light surfaces
- **20–30%:** Obsidian and dark surfaces
- **5–10%:** Cobalt
- **<5%:** Secondary UI colors

Cobalt should generally remain a minority color. The goal is for a user to notice cobalt immediately, not become visually desensitised to it.

---

## 5. Logo Usage

The BayesForce mark is the visual anchor of the identity.

### On Light Backgrounds
Use:
- **Cobalt mark + Obsidian wordmark**
- *This should be the default logo treatment.*

### On Dark Backgrounds
Use:
- **Cobalt mark + white/light wordmark**
- *The mark remains Cobalt.*

### Do Not
- Change the Cobalt to a generic gradient
- Add unnecessary glow effects to the logo
- Put the logo inside a decorative container without reason
- Distort or stretch the mark
- Introduce additional logo colors
- Use pure black when Obsidian is available
- Place the blue mark on a blue background with insufficient contrast

---

## 6. Typography

### Primary Display Typeface: Outfit
Outfit should carry the brand's visual personality.

**Use it for:**
- Hero headlines
- Page titles
- Section headings
- Navigation
- Buttons
- Labels
- Large statistics
- Short UI headings

**Recommended weights:**
- `400`: supporting display text
- `500`: navigation / UI
- `600`: section headings
- `600–700`: major hero headlines

*Avoid excessive use of 700/800 weights. The brand should feel confident through scale and spacing, not through unnecessarily heavy typography.*

### Secondary Typeface: Inter
Inter should handle information density.

**Use it for:**
- Body copy
- Descriptions
- Long-form content
- Case studies
- Forms
- Tables
- Technical specifications
- Metadata
- Supporting UI

**Recommended weights:**
- `400`: body
- `500`: emphasis
- `600`: small headings / controls

---

## 7. Typography Hierarchy

A typical hierarchy:

### Eyebrow
Small, uppercase or sentence-case label.  
*Example:* `AI ENGINEERING PARTNER`  
Use Cobalt or a muted light blue.

### Hero
Large Outfit headline.  
*Example:* **From AI ideas to real-world systems.**  
Use white on Obsidian. Highlight one important phrase in Cobalt.

### Supporting Copy
Inter, medium width, relatively restrained line length.  
Do not fill the hero with paragraphs. The website should feel like an engineering firm that knows what it does, not a company trying to explain everything immediately.

---

## 8. Hero Design

The hero should be the strongest expression of the visual system.

### Recommended Structure
- **Left:**
  - Eyebrow
  - Large headline
  - Supporting statement
  - Primary CTA
  - Secondary text link
- **Right:**
  - Abstract system visual
  - BayesForce mark geometry
  - AI pipeline
  - Network/system diagram
  - Data structure
  - Engineered 3D/2D form

*The visual should feel like a system being engineered, not generic AI imagery.*

### Hero Background
Preferred: `#11151B` or a very subtle Obsidian gradient.  
*Example conceptual gradient:*  
$$\text{Obsidian} \longrightarrow \text{slightly lighter Obsidian} \longrightarrow \text{Obsidian}$$

Cobalt can appear inside the visual rather than covering the entire background.

### Hero Lighting
Cobalt can be used as:
- Soft glow
- Edge illumination
- Gradient transition
- System node
- Data path
- Highlight
- Geometric surface

> [!NOTE]
> Glow should support geometry, not replace geometry. Avoid the stereotypical neon-AI aesthetic.

---

## 9. Cobalt Gradient Principles

Cobalt gradients may be used for visual depth.

**A useful progression:**
$$\#013\text{EFA} \longrightarrow \#2463\text{FF} \longrightarrow \#7\text{DB}0\text{FF}$$

**Use gradients primarily for:**
- Abstract hero visuals
- Data flows
- 3D system representations
- Interactive states
- Charts
- Decorative geometry

**Do not use gradients indiscriminately on:**
- Every heading
- Every button
- Every card
- Body text
- Logo

---

## 10. Buttons

### Primary CTA
- **Background:** `#013EFA`
- **Text:** `#FFFFFF`
- *Example:* `Get in touch →`
- **Recommended characteristics:**
  - Medium radius
  - Strong horizontal padding
  - Moderate height
  - Outfit / Inter medium weight
  - Minimal shadow
  - Subtle hover movement
- *The CTA should feel like an interface control, not a marketing badge.*

### Secondary CTA
- Preferred treatment: Transparent / white background with Obsidian border.
- *Examples:* `View case studies →` or `See our work →`
- On dark backgrounds, use a subtle white/blue outline.

---

## 11. Cards

Cards should remain restrained.

### Light Cards
- **Background:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Border:** `#E2E8F0`
- **Text:** `#11151B`
- **Icon:** `#013EFA`

### Dark Cards
- **Background:** `#161B23`
- **Border:** `#2A3441`
- **Text:** `#F8FAFC`
- **Accent:** `#013EFA`

Avoid excessive shadows. Prefer:
$$\text{border} + \text{spacing} + \text{contrast}$$
over:
$$\text{large drop shadow} + \text{rounded container} + \text{gradient}$$

---

## 12. Border Radius

The brand should use moderate rounding.

- **Buttons:** 8–12px
- **Cards:** 12–18px
- **Large visual containers:** 16–24px
- **Pills:** Only when semantically appropriate

Avoid making every component extremely rounded. The identity should retain an engineered, structured feel.

---

## 13. Iconography

Icons should feel technical and precise.

**Recommended characteristics:**
- Simple geometric construction
- Consistent stroke width
- Rounded or subtly softened joins
- Minimal internal detail
- Cobalt as the primary accent

**Conceptual categories:**
- Strategy $\rightarrow$ target / search
- Engineering $\rightarrow$ cube / system
- Deployment $\rightarrow$ cloud
- Data $\rightarrow$ graph / bars
- Integration $\rightarrow$ connected nodes
- Impact $\rightarrow$ measured output

*Avoid overly playful illustrations.*

---

## 14. Visual Language

The brand should develop its own visual vocabulary around:
1. **Systems:** Nodes, pipelines, modules and connected components.
2. **Flow:** Directional movement, data paths and transformation.
3. **Geometry:** Simple engineered forms.
4. **Signal:** Cobalt highlights indicating intelligence, action or important information.
5. **Structure:** Grids, layers, architecture and modular composition.

These five ideas become the foundation for illustrations, animations, case studies, and marketing visuals.

---

## 15. Photography

Photography should not be the primary brand language.

**When photography is used, prefer:**
- Architecture
- Industrial environments
- Data/operations environments
- Modern infrastructure
- Abstract technical environments
- Real-world systems

**Avoid generic:**
- Robot imagery
- Brain imagery
- Glowing circuit boards
- Humanoid AI imagery
- Stock photos of people looking at laptops

The company sells engineering capability, so the visual language should show systems and outcomes.

---

## 16. Case Study Design

Case studies should use Obsidian sections heavily.

### Example Structure:
- **Case study eyebrow:** `CASE STUDY`
- **Headline:** `From manual analysis to real-time intelligence.` *(Highlight the transformation phrase in Cobalt)*
- **Supporting information:** Short description.
- **System diagram:** Horizontal flow:  
  $$\text{Input} \longrightarrow \text{AI Processing} \longrightarrow \text{Intelligence} \longrightarrow \text{Business Outcome}$$  
  *Cobalt indicates movement through the system.*
- **Metrics:** Use large typography with restrained Cobalt highlights.  
  *Example:* `80%` with smaller label `Reduction in analysis time`. Metrics should be visually prominent but not overdecorated.

---

## 17. Navigation

Navigation should be extremely clean.

### Suggested Structure:
- **Left:** BayesForce
- **Center:** Services | Solutions | Case Studies | About
- **Right:** `Get in touch →`

### On Dark Hero:
- White navigation text
- Cobalt logo
- Cobalt CTA

### On Light Sections:
- Obsidian text
- Cobalt logo
- Cobalt CTA

---

## 18. Layout System

The layout should feel architectural.

- Wide desktop container
- Strong left alignment
- Generous horizontal margins
- Consistent grid
- Large vertical spacing
- Clear section boundaries

Avoid dense dashboard-like layouts for the marketing website. The visual impression should be:
$$\text{calm} \longrightarrow \text{precise} \longrightarrow \text{technical} \longrightarrow \text{premium}$$

---

## 19. Grid

Use a consistent grid system.

- **Recommended desktop:** 12-column grid
- Large outer margins
- 24–32px gutters

Use the grid to create alignment between:
- Headlines
- Cards
- Images
- Diagrams
- Metrics
- Navigation

*The grid should be visible through alignment, not through literal grid lines everywhere.*

---

## 20. Motion

Motion should communicate system behaviour.

### Good Animation Concepts:
- Data flowing between nodes
- Lines connecting
- Modules assembling
- Cobalt signal travelling through a system
- Charts progressively rendering
- Geometric surfaces shifting subtly
- Cards entering with small translations

### Avoid:
- Excessive parallax
- Constant floating elements
- Large bouncing animations
- Decorative particle fields
- Overly flashy transitions

*Motion should feel like software operating, not a marketing gimmick.*

---

## 21. Interaction States

- **Default:** Neutral surface.
- **Hover:** Small increase in contrast or Cobalt emphasis.
- **Active:** Cobalt.
- **Focus:** Visible Cobalt focus ring.
- **Disabled:** Muted Obsidian / slate.

Interaction should reinforce the same visual hierarchy as the static design.

---

## 22. Data Visualisation

Charts should primarily use:
- Cobalt
- Obsidian
- Slate
- Light surfaces

Cobalt should represent the important series or signal. Do not introduce ten unrelated colours unless the data genuinely requires them.

**For technical diagrams:**
- **Obsidian** = system
- **Cobalt** = intelligence / movement
- **White** = space / separation

---

## 23. Dark Mode

Dark mode is not simply an inversion.

- **Background:** `#080B0F` or `#11151B`
- **Elevated surface:** `#161B23`
- **Border:** `#2A3441`
- **Text:** `#F8FAFC`
- **Secondary text:** `#94A3B8`
- **Primary accent:** `#013EFA`

Cobalt should become more visually prominent against the dark background.

---

## 24. Light Mode

Light mode should feel like an engineering document rather than a generic SaaS dashboard.

- **Background:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Secondary surface:** `#F7F9FC`
- **Border:** `#E2E8F0`
- **Text:** `#11151B`
- **Secondary text:** `#64748B`
- **Accent:** `#013EFA`

Large areas of white space are encouraged.

---

## 25. What the Brand Should Feel Like

The intended emotional progression is:
$$\text{Technical} \longrightarrow \text{Precise} \longrightarrow \text{Confident} \longrightarrow \text{Intelligent} \longrightarrow \text{High-value}$$

Not:
$$\text{Playful} \longrightarrow \text{Futuristic} \longrightarrow \text{Experimental} \longrightarrow \text{Neon}$$

The distinction is critical: BayesForce is positioned as an engineering partner, so the identity must communicate capability before novelty.

---

## 26. What to Avoid

### Generic AI Aesthetics
- Purple-blue gradients everywhere
- Neon cyan
- Glowing brains
- Robots
- Neural-network stock imagery
- Generic AI particles

### Generic SaaS Aesthetics
- Excessive rounded cards
- Huge pill buttons
- Excessive shadows
- Every section looking like a dashboard
- Gradient text everywhere

### Generic Consulting Aesthetics
- Corporate navy everywhere
- Stock business photography
- Excessive text
- Decorative corporate diagrams
- Overly formal visual language

### Overuse of Cobalt
- Do not turn the website into a blue website.
- Cobalt is the signal.
- Obsidian and white provide the environment.

```text
                BAYESFORCE
                    │
        ┌───────────┴───────────┐
        │                       │
     OBSIDIAN                COBALT
    Foundation                Signal
        │                       │
   Authority              Intelligence
   Engineering               Action
   Systems                  Movement
   Depth                     Focus
        │                       │
        └───────────┬───────────┘
                    │

```

The three-part visual relationship is:
- **Obsidian** = foundation
- **Cobalt** = signal
- **White** = clarity

  ↓

  ↓

## 30. Brand Principle in One Sentence

> **BayesForce should look like the company you call when an AI idea needs to become a real, reliable system.**

The visual identity should therefore communicate engineering seriousness first, AI capability second, and visual novelty third.

---

## Final Design Direction

The recommended BayesForce identity is:

$$\mathbf{Cobalt\ Blue\ (\#013EFA)\ \times\ Obsidian\ Black\ (\#11151B)}$$

supported by restrained slate neutrals, white space, Outfit + Inter typography, geometric system visuals, and controlled Cobalt motion.

The result should feel:
**Premium. Technical. Precise. Modern. Confident.**

Not:
*Neon. Futuristic. Generic. Over-designed.*

This should be treated as the foundation for the website, pitch deck, proposals, case studies, social graphics, product interfaces, and future BayesForce marketing material.
