### 3. Sagar's Founder Traps & Structural Guardrails

To maximize winnability, we must proactively guard against Sagar's primary failure modes:

| Potential Founder Risk | Manifestation in Market Selection | Enforced Guardrail & Constraint |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Selection Paralysis (Too Many Interests)** | High curiosity allows rationalizing 10 different attractive businesses simultaneously. | **Rule:** Eliminate all markets outside the Top 11 Shortlist. Force single-wedge concentration before exploring adjacent verticals. |
| **2. Intellectualization & Over-Analysis** | Spending weeks designing the "perfect" agentic architecture before getting customer feedback. | **Rule:** Customer contact is the mandatory antidote. Must close first paid pilot before building complex multi-agent frameworks. |
| **3. Ambition Distorting Near-Term Economics** | Rejecting a high-margin ₹10 Cr business because it isn't an obvious $10B venture monopoly. | **Rule:** Prioritize Year 1 cash flow and ₹10–20 Cr ARR first. Treat this business as the foundational cash engine for the broader Bayes ecosystem. |

Sell to non-technical business buyers (CFOs, COOs, Claims Heads, General Counsels) who have budget and lack coding skills. |
