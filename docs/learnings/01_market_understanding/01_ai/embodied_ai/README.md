## Domain Definition

AI whose core product value requires sensing, deciding, or acting in the physical world through a machine — robots, drones, autonomous vehicles, and AI-native physical hardware.

**Boundary Inclusion:**  
Products where the physical hardware and its AI autonomy are inseparable from the value proposition; companies that must manufacture or certify physical machines.

**Boundary Exclusion:**  
Software used to manage physical assets (fleet management software belongs in Vertical Intelligence > Movement & Commerce). Simulation software without a physical product.
