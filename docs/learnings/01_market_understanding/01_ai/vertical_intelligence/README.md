## Domain Definition

AI products whose primary value derives from deep integration with the workflows, data, regulations, and distribution channels of a specific industry. The buyer is a domain professional, not a generic functional leader.

**Boundary Inclusion:**  
Products that would lose most of their value outside the target industry; products that require industry-specific datasets, regulatory credentials, or specialist sales motion.

**Boundary Exclusion:**  
AI tools bought by generic roles (legal, HR, finance) that happen to be in a specific company — if the tool works equally well across industries, it belongs in Horizontal Work.
