## Domain Definition

AI products whose primary value is adopted and experienced by an individual consumer or prosumer in their personal life — not their professional role in an organization.

**Boundary Inclusion:**  
Consumer-facing applications where the end-user pays directly or is the primary beneficiary; tools marketed to individuals rather than companies.

**Boundary Exclusion:**  
Professional tools used by individual employees (those belong in Horizontal Work or Vertical Intelligence). Business productivity tools that happen to have individual licenses.
