## Domain Definition

Products bought to create, train, connect, deploy, evaluate, secure, or operate AI systems. The direct customer is an AI builder — a developer, data scientist, ML engineer, or AI product team.

**Boundary Inclusion:**  
Infrastructure, platforms, tooling, and services whose value proposition is building or running AI rather than using AI for a business outcome.

**Boundary Exclusion:**  
AI-powered applications that use enabling-layer tools but whose primary value is a business outcome (belongs in Horizontal Work, Vertical Intelligence, Personal AI, or Embodied AI).
