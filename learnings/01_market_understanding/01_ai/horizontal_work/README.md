## Domain Definition

Cross-industry software that improves a broadly shared business function or knowledge workflow using AI. The buyer is a functional leader or knowledge worker, not an AI engineer and not an industry specialist.

**Boundary Inclusion:**  
AI tools sold to roles that exist in most companies — sales, marketing, legal, finance, HR, IT, operations — where the core value is workflow acceleration, not industry-specific knowledge.

**Boundary Exclusion:**  
Tools that require deep industry-specific data, regulatory knowledge, or specialist distribution channels (those belong in Vertical Intelligence). Tools for AI builders (Enabling AI). Consumer tools (Personal AI).
